---
name: Safety scoring
description: Durable safety boundary for the stress assessment product.
---

The direct self-harm screening item is stored and flagged independently from the wellness stress index; facial signals must never trigger crisis handling.

**Why:** Facial expression inference is not reliable enough for safety-critical decisions, and blending the item into a general stress score could obscure an urgent response.

**How to apply:** Keep crisis handling answer-driven, immediate, and visible before normal report content. Exclude the safety item from overall and category stress calculations.
